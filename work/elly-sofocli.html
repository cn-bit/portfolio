Selected work

Elly Sofoclí

A brand, website and wholesale programme that took a Melbourne bridal designer into four new markets.

Client
Elly Sofoclí, contemporary bridal, Melbourne
Scope
Brand identity, website design and build, content plan, go-to-market strategy, wholesale programme
My role
Account Director, Antimony Studio
Year
To confirm

Context

Elly Sofoclí designs bridal gowns with clean cuts, textured embellishment and a strong editorial point of view. The studio had built its reputation in Melbourne and wanted to reach brides and bridal salons well beyond it.

Challenge

International growth needed more than beautiful product. Bridal salons in new markets had to understand the label quickly, see a clear commercial offer and trust that a Melbourne studio could support them as a partner. The brand, the website and the wholesale proposition all had to tell the same story.

Approach

Antimony Studio developed the brand identity, designed and built a new website, and set out a content management plan so the label would present consistently across every channel.

Alongside this, a go-to-market strategy defined the wholesale partnership programme: the product offer for salons, wholesale pricing, the markets to approach first and the prospective partners within them.

My role

I led the client relationship and the commercial side of the programme, working with Elly and the studio team from strategy through to launch.

  • Client and stakeholder leadership across the engagement
  • Wholesale pricing and the product offer for partners
  • Research into prospective stockists in each market
  • Introductions to bridal salons and partners
  • Creative project management across brand, web and content

Brand, design and development by the Antimony Studio team. Elly's sales team led final negotiations with each partner.

Outcome

  • Queensland
  • New York
  • China
  • South Korea

The wholesale programme established seven new stockist relationships across four markets, including Mark Ingram in New York, Monarca in Shanghai and SOYOO Bridal in Seoul.

With the foundations in place, Elly and her team brought wholesale in-house and have continued to grow the accounts.

Elly Sofoclí

International Wholesale Development | Commercial Strategy | Brand & Digital Communications

Building an international wholesale proposition for a Melbourne bridal designer

Client
Elly Sofoclí, bridal design, Melbourne
Engagement
From December 2023
My role
Account Director, Antimony Studio
Outcome
Stockists opened in Queensland, New York, China and South Korea

The brief

Elly Sofoclí is a Melbourne bridal designer with an established retail presence. International wholesale offered a way to reach new brides through selected boutiques, but buyers needed a clear commercial proposition, the right product offering and a credible brand presentation, all without diluting the label's premium positioning.

Our approach

  • Wholesale proposition. Pricing strategy and a selected product offering for international stockists.
  • Market research. Identifying boutiques whose clientele and positioning suited the collection.
  • Introductions. Initial approaches to prospective stockists, for the client's sales team to progress.
  • Brand and digital. Brand identity, website and content for retail customers and wholesale buyers alike.

My contribution

I worked on the wholesale pricing strategy and product offering, researched suitable international retailers and made introductions to prospective stockists. Elly's sales team then led the final commercial discussions and closed the accounts.

I also managed the creation and rollout of supporting communications and design work, coordinating the studio's creative and digital specialists.

Services & deliverables

  • Wholesale market research
  • Wholesale pricing and product offering
  • Stockist identification and introductions
  • Brand identity development
  • Website design and development
  • Wholesale communications and creative

Outcome

  • Queensland
  • New York
  • China
  • South Korea

The programme set the commercial and communication foundations for international wholesale, opening stockist relationships in Queensland, New York, mainland China and South Korea. The label's later stockist network spans markets including the United States and Asia.

Research and introductions came through Antimony, with final negotiations managed by the client's sales team.

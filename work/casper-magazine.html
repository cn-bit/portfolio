Casper Magazine

B2B Go-to-Market Strategy | Commercial Product Development | Brand Partnerships & Digital Publishing

Building the commercial foundations of an independent digital publication

Client
Casper Magazine, digital publishing, Melbourne
Engagement
From March 2024
My role
Account Director, Antimony Studio
Outcome
140% more member sign-upsReported by Antimony Studio

The brief

Casper Magazine is an independent Melbourne publication covering fashion, beauty, wellness, design and culture, founded by its Editor-in-Chief, Maria Ugrinovski. It had a clear editorial voice and an engaged audience. To grow into a commercial digital publication, it needed a structured offer that advertisers could understand and buy, without compromising its editorial identity.

Our approach

  • Research. A review of comparable publications and how they package advertising and partnerships.
  • Commercial offer. A tiered package of digital placements, sponsored content, editorial collaborations and brand partnerships.
  • Media kit. The offer redesigned into a professional business development tool.
  • Brand and digital. Positioning, visual identity, website and editorial communications.
  • Partnerships and operations. Prospective advertisers, brand collaborations and new project management systems.

My contribution

As Account Director, I worked directly with Maria. I conducted the competitor research and, from it, developed the foundations of Casper's tiered advertising and content offering. I prepared and presented it to Maria, working with Antimony's Chief Strategist, who helped refine and approve the direction.

I led the media kit redevelopment, helped progress business-to-business partnerships and introduced project management systems that improved workflow across the engagement.

Branding, design, digital development and specialist creative work were delivered by the wider Antimony Studio team.

Services & deliverables

  • Competitor and market research
  • Tiered advertising and partnership offering
  • Media kit strategy, content and design
  • Brand identity and website
  • Business development and partnerships
  • Project management systems

Outcome

A clear advertising structure and media kit gave Casper a professional way to present its audience and opportunities, supporting new advertising avenues, brand collaborations and business-to-business partnerships.

Reported by Antimony Studio

  • 92% more website sessions
  • 140% more member sign-ups
  • 14 brand partner meetings

Antimony Studio reported a 92% increase in website sessions and a 75% increase in organic search traffic after the redesign, with member sign-ups up 140%. The advertiser pitch deck led to 14 meetings with prospective brand partners.

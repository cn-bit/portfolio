Femme Connection

Building a retention engine for an online fashion retailer.

Client
Femme Connection, online womenswear, Australia
Scope
Go-to-market planning, lifecycle email and SMS, loyalty and referrals, influencer and ambassador programmes, website optimisation
My role
Account Director, then Client Services Director, Antimony Studio
Year
2024 to 2026

Context

Femme Connection is an Australian online fashion retailer offering accessible, current womenswear, from occasion dresses to workwear and seasonal collections.

Challenge

Like many online retailers, the business leaned on paid acquisition to drive sales. The opportunity was to keep more of the customers it had already won: bring first-time buyers back, reward loyal ones and give the brand a community beyond the checkout.

Approach

The work started with brand foundations and a go-to-market plan, then built a retention programme around them. Klaviyo email and SMS flows covered every stage from welcome to abandoned cart, checkout, cross-sell and post-purchase, with A/B tests running across each sequence.

Yotpo loyalty tiers and referrals rewarded repeat customers, while influencer, ambassador and affiliate programmes extended the brand's reach. The website went through several rounds of redesign focused on conversion, cart performance and purchase pathways, and monthly reporting tied every channel back to revenue.

My role

I led the client relationship for nearly two years and managed the email channel directly, working with the studio team on strategy, design and reporting.

  • Client leadership and monthly performance reporting
  • Lifecycle email and SMS strategy in Klaviyo
  • Loyalty and referral programme with Yotpo
  • Influencer, ambassador and affiliate partnerships
  • Campaign planning across the retail calendar
  • Website conversion improvements

Design, development and campaign production by the Antimony Studio team.

Outcome

  • Flow revenue up 60% year on year

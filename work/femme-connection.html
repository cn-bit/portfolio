Femme Connection

E-commerce | CRM & Lifecycle Marketing | Loyalty | Integrated Campaigns

Building an integrated customer retention and digital growth programme

Client
Femme Connection, online womenswear, Australia
Engagement
2024 to 2026
My role
Account Director, then Client Services Director, Antimony Studio
Outcome
$30K to $120K a monthEmail channel revenue

The brief

Femme Connection is an Australian online fashion retailer in a fast-moving market. Paid acquisition mattered, but growth also depended on converting and keeping the customers it already had. The engagement became an ongoing programme connecting acquisition, website experience, CRM, loyalty and reporting, with a regular rhythm for reviewing results and setting priorities.

Our approach

  • CRM and lifecycle. Klaviyo email and SMS flows for welcome, abandoned cart, checkout, post-purchase and cross-sell, refined through QA and A/B testing.
  • Loyalty. Yotpo rewards and referral strategy to support repeat purchasing.
  • Brand and e-commerce. Brand identity, website work, conversion review and Shopify development scoping.
  • Campaigns and partnerships. Retail calendar campaigns alongside influencer, ambassador and affiliate activity.
  • Reporting. Regular reviews connecting performance data with practical next steps.

My contribution

I led the senior client relationship and coordinated the programme across the client and studio teams. I was directly responsible for email and CRM: campaign planning, lifecycle strategy, creative reviews, flow QA, testing priorities and performance analysis.

I ran the reporting and review cadence, and coordinated work across website, loyalty, content, optimisation and partnerships, including Shopify migration scoping and loyalty strategy.

Design, development and specialist creative production were delivered collaboratively by Antimony Studio.

Services & deliverables

  • Klaviyo email and SMS marketing
  • Lifecycle flows, QA and A/B testing
  • Yotpo loyalty and referral strategy
  • Brand identity and e-commerce experience
  • Campaigns and partnerships
  • Shopify scoping and performance reporting

Outcome

  • $30,000 to $120,000 a month

Email became a major contributor to revenue. Over the engagement, monthly email revenue grew from around $30,000 to $120,000, and in January 2026 automated flow revenue was 60% higher than a year earlier. The client gained clear visibility of performance and the next priorities.

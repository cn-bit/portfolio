RT Edgar

Brand Transformation | Customer Experience | Digital Strategy & Delivery

An established real estate group, reimagined for a changing market

Client
RT Edgar, real estate, Melbourne
Engagement
From September 2024
My role
Account Director, then Client Services Director, Antimony Studio
Outcome
Customer research and strategy that shaped the new identity and website

The brief

Founded in 1894, RT Edgar is one of Melbourne's established premium residential agencies. Its reputation was a real asset, but customers met the business through many channels: listings, portals, offices, agents, print and the website. The task was to modernise the brand and digital experience without losing the trust built over generations, and to make every touchpoint feel like one business.

Our approach

  • Research and CX. A competitor audit, audience segmentation, personas and journey mapping to set priorities.
  • Brand. A refreshed identity, including the RTE monogram, applied across brochures, sales boards and signage.
  • Website. A new site bringing identity, content and property search together, coordinated with CMS, data and API partners.
  • Content. Content planning and video production to carry the brand consistently.

My contribution

I led the client relationship and the research and strategy phase. I conducted the competitor audit and developed the audience segmentation, buyer personas and journey mapping. I then worked with Antimony's Chief Strategist and Marketing Coordinator to turn the findings into the CX recommendations that set priorities across brand and digital.

Through delivery, I kept RT Edgar's stakeholders, the studio and external technical partners aligned, managing briefs, reviews and the dependencies between brand, content and website.

Brand design, development and creative production were delivered by the wider Antimony Studio team.

Services & deliverables

  • Research, segmentation and journey mapping
  • CX strategy and recommendations
  • Brand identity refresh
  • Website strategy, design and build
  • Content and video production
  • Print, sales boards and signage

Outcome

Brand, customer experience and digital, previously run as separate workstreams, now work as one programme: a refreshed identity, a new website and a consistent suite of marketing across the business, grounded in research I led.

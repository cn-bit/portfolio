RT Edgar

Bringing a Melbourne real estate group with 130 years of history into a contemporary brand and digital presence.

Client
RT Edgar, premium real estate, Melbourne
Scope
Research, monogram and lock-up redesign, website design and build, brochures, sales boards
My role
Account Director, Antimony Studio
Year
To confirm

Context

RT Edgar is a premium Melbourne real estate group with a history spanning 130 years. Its name carries real weight in the city's established suburbs, built office by office and generation by generation.

Challenge

The brand had to respect that heritage while presenting a contemporary, coherent and commercially useful presence across every office, listing and channel. The 130th year gave the work a clear moment to land.

Approach

The programme began with research: a competitor audit, audience segmentation and customer journey mapping. Those findings shaped a redesigned RTE monogram and lock-up, a new website designed and built by the studio, and a family of brochures and sales boards that carry the refreshed identity into every campaign.

A content and channel plan, built around the 130-year milestone, then carried the new direction across the group's channels.

My role

I led the client relationship across the broader programme, keeping stakeholders at RT Edgar and the studio team aligned from research through to launch.

  • Client and stakeholder leadership across the programme
  • Brand refresh, website design and development
  • Customer journey mapping and CX strategy
  • Strategic recommendations from journey research
  • Content and video production oversight
  • Coordination across web, content and digital delivery
